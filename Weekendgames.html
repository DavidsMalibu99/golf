Wolf — Round Setup

Wolf rotates automatically in tee order for holes 1-15 — each Wolf can't repeat a partner they've already picked. On holes 16-18, the Wolf is whoever is down the most money, with no partner restriction. Team score = best ball. Each player on the losing side pays the hole value to each player on the winning side — so Wolf + partner beating the other 3 collects $3 each at a $1 hole value. Lone Wolf pays/collects double. Ties push.

Blind Draw
Buy-in: $20.00 per player
Pay 2nd place — 1st 70% / 2nd 30%
Leave unchecked for winner take all. Applies to Front 9, Back 9 and the Par 3 / 4 / 5 pots. Blind Draw always pays only the winning team.
Number of groups: 3
Points: Eagle+=6, Birdie=4, Par=2, Bogey=1, Double Bogey=0, Triple Bogey or worse=-1. Your target per 9 is (36 - handicap) / 2, based on gross score (no per-hole strokes). Front 9 and back 9 are scored separately — whoever beats their own target by the most wins that 9. Par 3 pot is lowest combined gross on the course's par 3s — gross only, no strokes. Par 4 and Par 5 pots are lowest combined NET on those holes — strokes are given off the card's stroke index. Blind Draw pairs everyone at random after the cards are in; the two partners' margins add together and the best team splits the pot. Front 9 / Back 9 ties go to a card playoff, same as Wednesday night: fewest strokes on the hardest hole first, by the course's handicap order, within that nine — it settles 2nd place too. Par 3 / 4 / 5 ties split: if guys tie for 1st they split the whole pot evenly; with 2nd place on, a clear 1st gets 70% and anyone tied for 2nd splits the 30%. Uncheck any pot you're not playing for.
